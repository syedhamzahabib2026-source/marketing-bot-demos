FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions from Chicago property owners and managers about roofing services.

How long do roofs last?
Standard roof systems such as TPO, PVC, EPDM, and asphalt shingles have an expected service life of around 20 years. You can expect a 40 to 70-year lifespan from metal roofing. Concrete, slate, and clay roofing are the longest-lasting materials, with life expectancies over 100 years. All roofing materials require proper care and routine maintenance to ensure they reach their full potential.
If my roof is leaking, do I have to replace it?
No — a leaking roof does not automatically require full replacement. A thorough inspection by our team will identify the exact cause of the leak and determine the most cost-effective solution. In many cases a targeted repair is all that is needed. Unless your roof has reached the end of its service life or sustained severe structural damage, we will always explore repair options first.
Does my insurance cover storm roof damage?
In many cases, yes. If your roof was damaged by rain, snow, wind, or hail, your property insurance policy may cover the cost of repairs or replacement. Durable Roofing Co works directly with your insurance adjuster to document all damage thoroughly, helping to maximize your claim and minimize out-of-pocket expenses.
What types of commercial roofing systems do you install?
We install and service a comprehensive range of commercial roofing systems including flat roofs, steep slope roofs, TPO, PVC, EPDM single-ply membranes, metal roofing, fluid-applied coatings, and sustainable roofing solutions. As certified installers for the industry's most trusted brands, every installation is backed by full manufacturer warranties.
How quickly can you respond to an emergency roofing call?
We prioritize fast response for all emergency roofing situations. Members of our preventative maintenance programs receive priority emergency service. For all other clients, we work to respond as quickly as possible — call us at 708-371-7663 for the fastest response.
Do you offer free estimates?
Yes — Durable Roofing Co provides free estimates and evaluations to all clients. Our accurate, no-obligation estimates help you understand exactly what your roofing project requires and allow you to budget accordingly before committing to any work.
