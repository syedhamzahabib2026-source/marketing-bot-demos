Our Story

STARTED WITH A
HOT DOG STAND.
NOW WE'RE LEGENDARY.

In 1963, Dick Portillo started selling hot dogs out of a 6-by-12-foot trailer with no running water, heat, or bathroom — just a dream and a $1,100 investment. Over 60 years later, that obsession with quality has grown into one of America's most beloved restaurant brands.
