A Chicago Original

Handcrafted Tradition.
Legendary Flavor.

For more than 50 years, the Malnati family has been perfecting Chicago's deep dish tradition in their pizzerias—built on quality ingredients and time-honored recipes. What started in Lincolnwood, Illinois in 1971 continues today, still driven by family, pride, and true Chicago spirit.

"We've stayed true to the original Chicago-style Deep Dish pizza recipe that Grandpa Malnati helped create in 1943 at Chicago's first Deep Dish pizzeria."
