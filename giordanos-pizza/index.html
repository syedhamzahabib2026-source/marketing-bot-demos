What Makes Us Different

The Architecture of Our Deep Dish

We don't cut corners. Every ingredient is chosen with obsessive care — because the best pizza starts with the best ingredients.

🧀

Wisconsin Mozzarella

Our signature mozzarella is specially produced for Giordano's by skilled Wisconsin cheese makers using only the finest milk from local farmers. Aged to perfection and freshly shredded in-house every day for that legendary Giordano's melt.

🍅

Hand-Picked Tomatoes

We use only the finest tomatoes in our sauce, freshly prepared in-house each day. Exclusively grown in Mendocino County, Northern California — renowned for producing some of the world's best tomatoes, nurtured by local farmers and picked at peak ripeness.

🌾

Midwest Flour

Our dough starts with flour from a single mill in Minnesota — unbleached and a special proprietary blend of North and South Dakota wheat. This is what gives our legendary crust its distinctive flaky, buttery texture that can't be replicated.
