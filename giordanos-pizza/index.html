What Makes Us Different

The Architecture of Our Deep Dish

Every ingredient is sourced with obsessive care. Here's what goes into every legendary Giordano's pizza.

🧀

Wisconsin Mozzarella

Specially produced for Giordano's by skilled Wisconsin cheesemakers using only the finest milk from local farms. Aged to perfection and freshly shredded in-house every day for that legendary Giordano's melt.

🍅

Hand-Picked Tomatoes

Exclusively grown in Mendocino County, Northern California — one of the world's best tomato regions. Nurtured by local farmers and carefully hand-picked at peak ripeness. Freshly prepared as sauce in-house every day.

🌾

Midwest Flour

Sourced from a single mill in Minnesota. Unbleached, with a proprietary blend of North and South Dakota wheat — crafted specifically to create the iconic flaky, golden Giordano's crust you know and love.
