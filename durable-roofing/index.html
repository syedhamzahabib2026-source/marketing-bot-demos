What To Do After a Storm

1

Roof Inspection

Durable Roofing performs detailed inspections and certifications to locate, photograph, and document all storm damage on your roof — providing a complete record for your insurance claim.

2

Insurance Claims

Rain, snow, wind, or hail damage may be covered by your building's insurance policy. We work alongside your adjuster and guide you through the claim process to minimise your out-of-pocket costs.

3

Storm Damage Repairs
