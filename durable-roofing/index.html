Common Questions

Straight answers from Durable Roofing's team — no sales pitch, just facts.

How long do roofs typically last?

Standard systems like TPO, PVC, EPDM, and asphalt shingles have an expected service life of around 20 years. Metal roofing runs 40 to 70 years. Concrete, slate, and clay materials can last over 100 years. All materials require proper care and routine maintenance to reach their full potential.

If my roof is leaking, does it need to be replaced?

Not necessarily. A thorough inspection identifies the root cause of the leak and determines whether a targeted repair will solve it. Replacement is only recommended when a roof has reached the end of its service life or when damage is too extensive to repair cost-effectively. We will always give you an honest assessment.

Do you offer maintenance plans?

Yes. Durable Roofing offers preventative maintenance plans for commercial, multi-unit, and industrial properties. Members receive priority scheduling and emergency response, plus regular inspections to catch small problems before they become costly repairs.

Is the estimate really free?

Completely. We provide free estimates and evaluations to help you understand the scope of any needed work and budget accordingly. There is no obligation — just an honest, accurate assessment from an experienced roofer.
